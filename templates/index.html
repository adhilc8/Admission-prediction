How to read these results

A smaller KEAM rank is better. “Within 2025 cutoff” means your rank is numerically less than or equal to the category closing rank in the published 2025 first phase. The optional 10% range is only for exploration. Missing category ranks are omitted. Actual allotments depend on current-year ranks, options submitted, seats, reservation rules, and allotment phase.
